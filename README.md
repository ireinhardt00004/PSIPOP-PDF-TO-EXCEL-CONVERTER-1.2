# PSIPOP PDF TO EXCEL CONVERTER 1.2

The PSIPOP PDF to Excel Converter (PSIPOP-PTEC) was developed to support Human Resource Management Officers (HRMOs), administrative personnel, and other authorized users in efficiently processing Personnel Services Itemization and Plantilla of Personnel (PSIPOP) documents. The system automates the conversion of PSIPOP PDF files into structured Microsoft Excel workbooks, ensuring that extracted personnel information remains accurate, complete, and consistent. The application is maintained and continuously enhanced by the developer to ensure compatibility with current PSIPOP document formats and to provide technical support whenever necessary. Through automation, the system promotes a more efficient workflow and improves the overall management of plantilla records.

Before the implementation of PSIPOP-PTEC, the conversion of PSIPOP documents was performed entirely through manual encoding. Personnel first downloaded PSIPOP PDF files from the Department of Budget and Management (DBM)-PSIPOP website and manually transferred the information into Microsoft Excel spreadsheets. This process typically required three to four days to complete, depending on the volume of records, and demanded meticulous verification to ensure the accuracy of employee information, salary data, item numbers, plantilla assignments, and other personnel details. The manual procedure frequently resulted in formatting inconsistencies, duplicate entries, omitted records, and other encoding errors that delayed report preparation and increased administrative workload.

The implementation of PSIPOP-PTEC has transformed this process by automating the extraction and organization of data directly from PSIPOP PDF documents. After uploading a PDF file, users can instantly extract the information, review the converted records through an interactive preview, perform keyword searches to validate specific entries, and export the results into a standardized Microsoft Excel workbook within a matter of minutes. Compared with the previous manual approach, the system delivers substantial improvements in speed, accuracy, consistency, and reliability while significantly reducing the time and effort required for data preparation. As a result, HR personnel can focus more on validating personnel information, preparing reports, and performing other essential administrative responsibilities instead of repetitive manual data encoding.
